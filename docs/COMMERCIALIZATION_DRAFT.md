## 1. Operating model and current state

The proposed model is free source inspection; a free 30-day organizational evaluation; paid internal or professional operational use; customer-operated installations; and a separately released, genuinely open-source report-verification component only after its rights and implementation are cleared.

NICO retains one assessment-quality standard. Price or license scope must not change evidence truth, known limitations, score meaning, authorization, required human review, or exact-edition delivery controls.

The verified repository documents currently describe source-available, noncommercial licensing; commercial use by separate agreement; local/no-server and hosted workflows; and required expert review before client delivery. Documentation is not proof that every described feature is currently qualified in production.

The commercial draft must remain isolated from the existing repair branches. PR #1627 covers unfinished C/C++ capability and Bitcoin qualification. PR #1632 covers report corrections. Neither is to be modified, merged, rebased, replaced, or represented as production-qualified by this commercial task.

## 2. Proposed evaluation license

**Draft replacement policy for legal review. It is not a grant of rights.**

### 2.1 Parties and covered software

The current license names Cody Jenkins / BoneManTGRM as licensor. Confirm the contracting legal name and address before finalization; this package does not establish a new entity or claim ownership of third-party material. The covered software is the NICO release identified in the final terms, excluding customer-provided materials and third-party components subject to separate terms.

### 2.2 Free inspection and noncommercial use

Permit source and documentation inspection without a sales conversation. Permit personal, educational, research, and defensive noncommercial use on owned or explicitly authorized targets, subject to the final source-available terms. Public availability is not a representation that the full platform is open source. Existing rights under platform terms, prior licenses, or third-party licenses are not extinguished by these proposed terms.

### 2.3 Explicit organizational evaluation exception

Notwithstanding any definition that otherwise classifies business or government use as commercial, permit an organization to install, run, and privately modify the covered release solely to evaluate suitability for 30 consecutive 24-hour periods from its first evaluation execution. Record the start and end in UTC. Source inspection alone does not start the period.

This exception may provide internal evaluation value to the organization; that value alone does not make the permitted trial a prohibited commercial use. One initial evaluation period applies per organization unless the licensor expressly extends it. Reinstallation, a release update, additional users, or a different machine do not restart the same period.

Permitted evaluation includes authorized test assessments, internal reviewer comparison, reproducibility checks, testing integrations, and private experimental modifications. An employee or controlled contractor may act for the evaluating organization under equivalent confidentiality and scope restrictions.

Evaluation excludes routine operational assessments, paid or unpaid client deliverables, resale, public hosted access, managed services, and embedding the platform in a distributed product. A consulting firm evaluating the software may use authorized test targets but may not use the trial to fulfill a client engagement.

### 2.4 Costs, support, and expiry

The evaluating organization pays its own infrastructure, model/API, scanner, and other third-party costs. No credit card, subscription, automatic payment, or automatic renewal is created by this proposed software trial. No included support, deployment work, or service-level commitment is promised.

At expiry, stop executing the software under the evaluation permission or obtain an appropriate commercial license. The evaluator may retain generated evaluation results, permitted audit records, and one nonexecuted archival copy for legal or compliance needs, subject to confidentiality. Retention does not grant ongoing execution rights. Rights separately held under another valid license continue according to that license.

### 2.5 Safety, reports, and intellectual property

Evaluation is not authorization to assess a third party's systems. The operator must establish target authorization and preserve NICO's safety, review, and delivery controls. Automated drafts may be examined internally for evaluation but must not be represented as expert-approved, client-ready, or a security certification.

The evaluator retains its preexisting rights in supplied source code, information, and other customer materials. The evaluator may retain evaluation reports and share them internally with authorized reviewers. No report recipient needs a software license merely to read a lawfully shared report. Rights in underlying NICO templates and third-party content remain distinct from report-use permission.

Private modifications are not automatically assigned to the licensor. Submitted contributions require an explicit contribution-rights arrangement; do not imply that a blanket ownership claim captures all independent customer work.

### 2.6 Final legal provisions

The final license should retain appropriately reviewed warranty disclaimers, liability provisions, material-breach termination and cure rules, rights reservations, and a specified governing-law/dispute process. No zero-liability guarantee is made. Do not incorporate unresolved draft provisions by reference into an accepted customer agreement.

## 3. Proposed commercial agreement

**Draft agreement structure. No rights arise until a complete order and final terms are accepted by both authorized parties.**

### 3.1 Grant and two standard scopes

Subject to the accepted order and payment terms, grant a nonexclusive, nontransferable license for the identified organization, release, deployment scope, and permitted users. The initial offer is continuing use of the specifically identified verified release, subject to material-breach termination; it is not ownership of NICO or entitlement to every future release.

**Internal use:** Authorized assessments of the licensee's own systems. Named employees and controlled contractors may operate NICO for that purpose. Private modifications are permitted within the licensed scope.

**Professional use:** Internal-use rights plus authorized third-party client assessments and the right to charge for reviewed assessment services and deliver approved reports to those clients. The purchased organizational scope, not a separate permission request for each engagement, controls use. The firm must still obtain authorization from each target owner and appoint qualified reviewers.

Professional use does not automatically include giving clients interactive access to NICO, software redistribution, sublicensing, resale, white-label software, embedding in another distributed product, or operating a public hosted service. Those require separately negotiated rights. Running the firm's assessment service on its own private infrastructure is permitted within the professional scope; this is distinct from reselling software access.

### 3.2 Exact release and continuing rights

Identify the licensed source commit, packaged release, checksums, deployment materials, and documented limitations in the order or delivery schedule. A continuing-use license does not expire merely because optional support or update purchases stop. Lawfully made private modifications remain usable within that continuing scope.

No promise is made that a historical release will remain secure, compatible, or suitable indefinitely. The customer is responsible for its operating environment and patch strategy. Distinguish continued legal use rights from technical support and maintenance availability.

### 3.3 Data and report-use rights

The customer retains its rights in supplied code, evidence, and confidential information. It grants only the limited processing permission needed for any specifically agreed service. There is no general training or secondary-use permission for private customer materials.

The customer receives continuing rights to retain, use, and share lawfully generated reports with recipients authorized under its scope and confidentiality obligations. Professional customers may deliver reviewed reports to their authorized clients. Report-use rights survive the end of optional support and update arrangements; they do not permit distributing underlying platform source code or representing an unapproved draft as an approved report.

Public case studies, testimonials, customer names, logos, and report publication require separate permission. Customer permission does not substitute for target authorization, redaction, or expert approval.

### 3.4 Ownership and contributions

Each party retains its preexisting intellectual property. No assignment of NICO ownership or exclusive commercialization right is made. Rights in commissioned work, privately developed extensions, feedback, and contributions must be explicitly specified; do not automatically equate a license to use a contribution with ownership of it.

Third-party components retain their own notices and obligations. Do not promise rights broader than the licensor can grant. A customer modification does not become an official NICO release or approved report without the relevant release/review process.

### 3.5 Customer operation and bounded handover

The customer operates its installation, pays infrastructure and external-service charges, controls credentials, manages backups and recovery, handles client support, and performs required human report review. The customer names a technical operator before handover.

Support is **none included** unless the order expressly purchases a defined service. The order must state any handover deliverables, hours cap, channels, acceptance criteria, and exclusions. No unlimited remediation, custom development, deployment success guarantee, incident response, future integration, or 24/7 service is implied.

Future releases may be separately offered; there is no obligation to create them. A maintenance partnership requires a separate funded scope, named maintainer, release responsibilities, and agreed contribution rights. A customer's responsibility for its installation is not a commitment to maintain NICO's shared codebase.

### 3.6 Payment and risk allocation

Prices are by owner-approved written quote; no amount has been approved in this package. An order must state fee, currency, payment schedule, applicable tax treatment, and any separately purchased services. Do not activate billing or treat a missing price as zero.

Use a material-breach process with notice and an appropriate cure period in the final agreement; specify treatment of serious unauthorized use and surviving confidentiality/report rights. Qualified counsel must finalize warranty exclusions, liability caps and exceptions, any indemnity, remedies, governing law, and dispute resolution. Do not assert that a disclaimer eliminates all liability or that the agreement is enforceable everywhere.

## 5. Five-firm pilot and evaluation workflow

### 5.1 Two separate routes

**Self-hosted software evaluation:** Once final evaluation terms are approved and effective, an eligible organization can follow the public terms without a sales negotiation. It supplies its own infrastructure and records the evaluation start. This draft does not implement a licensing server, timer, tracking pixel, or automatic entitlement.

**Owner-operated hosted pilot:** Application-based, selected by the owner, with no automatic access to NICO's protected infrastructure. This is a separate engagement permission, not a loophole in the evaluation restriction on client deliverables. Any pilot report is subject to the existing exact-edition expert-review and delivery-authorization process. Selection does not certify the release or preapprove report delivery.

### 5.2 Minimal application fields

Collect organization name, contact name and work email, internal/professional interest, a short intended-use description, and the preferred self-hosted/hosted route. A website or public repository URL is optional; blank optional fields remain “Not supplied.” Do not request private source code, credentials, sensitive findings, or private access tokens on the public form.

The application is an expression of interest only. It does not attest target authorization, sign a license, start an assessment, approve a report, create an invoice, or opt the applicant into unrelated marketing. Confirm target permission later through the existing authorized intake before actual assessment.

### 5.3 Handling and safeguards

Use a private owner-controlled destination approved before collection begins. Identify the data controller/contact, purposes, retention policy, processors, and applicable rights in an approved privacy notice. Do not invent a contact address or infer one from commit metadata. Do not store applicant records in this public repository.

For a live endpoint: enforce bounded input and request sizes, validation, rate limiting, anti-abuse protection, private storage, idempotent submission, and redacted logs. Return success only after durable receipt. On network/storage failure show “Not submitted” and allow a safe retry; do not silently discard applications or claim success from a front-end-only form. Marketing consent, where used, must be separate and optional.

### 5.4 Operator procedure

Record application received, owner selection, confirmed scope, named operator/reviewer, and applicable terms. Choose five suitable firms before expanding the program. Never represent empty slots as accepted customers.

For selected pilots, confirm the qualified release, authorization, allowed cost/budget, and output scope. Run the assessment through existing controls, record reviewer corrections and limitations, and deliver only the legitimately approved edition to authorized recipients.

At evaluation end, collect repeated-use evidence, observed reviewer time/corrections, operational burden, missing requirements, and an explicit response to the actual commercial quote. A compliment, download, or free assessment is not evidence of a sale. Case-study consent is separate from participation.

## 7. NICO Verify: bounded release specification

**Specification only. No implementation, compatibility test, extraction, Apache grant, package publication, or trademark registration is claimed.**

The proposed verifier should run locally without sending source, evidence, or reports to the licensor. It should not require the assessment platform, a paid account, a hosted login, or model calls. Begin with existing canonical exports only after inspecting and documenting their actual schema. Do not invent a second authoritative report schema merely to make a demo pass.

### Minimum checks and outcomes

Check format/version support; required identities; bounded input parsing; declared artifact existence, size, and checksums; and consistency among the recorded repository commit, run, report, and approval references where those fields actually exist in the supported export. Missing fields must remain missing or unverifiable, not inferred.

Use separate outcomes for per-check pass, mismatch, missing input, unsupported version, and unverifiable claim. A package with both a changed report and a recomputed manifest can still be internally consistent. Without an independently trusted signature or reference digest, do not claim authenticity or tamper-proof origin.

A recorded approval flag is not proof that a qualified person actually reviewed anything. A checksum is not a security certification. A recorded commit identity is not proof that all source files were analyzed. Preserve those distinctions in machine output, documentation, and user-facing wording.

### Safety and regression requirements

Use synthetic fixtures; bound file count, total bytes, nesting, and processing time. Reject duplicate JSON keys, path traversal, absolute/outside-root paths, symlink escapes, ambiguous duplicate archive members, malformed/truncated data, and unsupported algorithms. Never execute analyzed project code, fetch untrusted URLs, or render report HTML with active content during verification.

Test an unchanged supported package, single-byte tampering, missing files, mismatched identities, unknown format versions, altered approval references, malicious paths, and a consistently rewritten unsigned package that must remain unauthenticated. Maintain the distinction between internal consistency and independent authenticity. Record exact test commands and fixtures before calling it ready.

### Licensing and extraction gate

Apache 2.0 is a proposed license for the isolated component, not permission granted by this document. Inventory copied code, contributors, dependency licenses, schemas, notices, and historical permissions. Review compatibility and rights to relicense. Obtain explicit owner approval for the exact file set and release, then include the proper license/notices and accurate package metadata in that isolated distribution. Do not replace NICO's root license or add commercial-use restrictions to a component advertised as Apache-licensed.

Future users, including competitors, could commercially use and redistribute a properly Apache-licensed verifier under its terms. The owner's approval must acknowledge that tradeoff. Establish a maintainer and vulnerability-reporting path before public release; do not promise unstaffed support.
